Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the QuickSheet for Papierkram Chrome Extension (hereinafter referred to as "the Extension").

1. Data Collection and Usage

The Extension operates under a strict "Local Only" privacy model. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal data, timesheets, or authentication credentials to any external servers.

All processing of your Excel timesheet files and interactions with your Papierkram.de account happen securely and entirely locally within your browser.

2. Data Stored Locally

To provide a seamless experience, the Extension stores the following data locally on your device using the browser's built-in chrome.storage.local API:

  • Your custom column and project mapping templates.
  • Incomplete timesheet import drafts (to prevent data loss if you close the extension).
  • Your selected language preferences.

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser's local data.

3. Interaction with Papierkram.de

The Extension communicates directly with your active Papierkram.de session to fetch existing projects/tasks and to submit your time entries. This communication happens directly between your browser and the Papierkram.de servers. We do not act as a middleman and have no access to this data traffic.

4. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party cookies in this Extension.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the Extension's data practices, please contact the developer via the support tab on the Chrome Web Store.
